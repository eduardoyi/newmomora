The photos are on your phone. The little things are harder to keep.

The question from the back seat. The word they say their own way. What was happening just before everyone started laughing.

Momora brings your photos, stories and recordings together, so there’s more to look back on than a camera roll.

START WITH THE PHOTOS YOU HAVE
You don’t have to fill an empty journal from scratch. With your permission, Momora looks through your photos, groups them into suggested memories and drafts captions. Keep the ones you like, change a few words, or leave them out. Your camera roll stays as it is.

FOR THE THINGS A PHOTO MISSED
Write a few words or say what happened while it’s still fresh. No need to make it sound special. “He packed crackers in case the playground got hungry” is already worth keeping.

You can turn a story into an AI illustration, too, using your family’s character portraits.

KEEP THEIR LITTLE VOICE
The way they say “banana.” Their laugh. The song they keep making up. Save the recording itself and listen again—not just a transcript of the words.

SOMETHING TO COME BACK TO
Keep memories together for each child. Look back through the photos and stories, play a familiar recording, and invite family members to share in it. You choose who joins your family journal.

AND A BOOK TO HOLD, TOO
When you’d like a printed baby book, choose a child and a time period. Momora selects saved memories, groups related moments and lays out the pages with your photos and stories. Review the book online, make changes and order a printed copy delivered to your door. Printed books are optional and purchased separately, with shipping extra.

YOUR MEMORIES STAY YOURS
Your saved memories remain available to read after your subscription ends. The family owner can export the archive, too.

Start with what you already have. Add the little things when you have a minute.
